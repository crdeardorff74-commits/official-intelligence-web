ABOUT & CONTACT

Official Intelligence is the personal game label of Christopher Ryan Deardorff — one person, working alone. I make free, browser-based games and experiments, including TANTЯO and Circuitousness, with more on the way.

Who I Am

My name is Christopher Ryan Deardorff (I go by Ryan). I am an independent solo developer based in the United States. I design, write the code for, test, and publish every game on this site myself, using AI tools as assistants along the way.

Official Intelligence is simply the name I publish under — a personal label, the way a musician might use a band name. To be completely explicit about it:

  • It is not a company. There is no incorporated business, no LLC, no staff, no investors, and no office — just me.
  • It is not a government agency, public body, or official organization of any kind, despite how the words "Official Intelligence" may sound. The name is a joke about AI, nothing more.
  • It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of any government, company, brand, or organization.
  • I am personally responsible for this website, every game on it, and any advertising that points to them.

What I Make

Polished, free-to-play puzzle and arcade games that run right in your browser — no download required, no purchase, and no ads inside the games. Each game also works as an installable progressive web app (PWA) so you can add it to your home screen. I do not sell anything, and there is nothing to buy.
